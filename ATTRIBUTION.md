# Attribution

This device shows Caltrain departures. It is built entirely on **data that other
organisations produce, own and publish**, under licences that require them to be
acknowledged. This file is that acknowledgment, stated in full rather than as a
footnote, because two of the four items below are contractual obligations rather
than courtesies.

---

## 1. Real-time departures — 511 SF Bay

**Live arrival predictions come from 511 SF Bay**, operated by the Metropolitan
Transportation Commission (MTC), via its SIRI `StopMonitoring` endpoint at
`api.511.org`.

> **Data provided by 511 SF Bay (511.org).**

That acknowledgment is **required**. The 511 Data Disseminator Agreement makes
acknowledgment of 511.org as the data provider a condition of use, in exchange
for a "nonexclusive, royalty-free, worldwide, non-transferable license to use,
sublicense, copy, distribute, and store the Provided Data; to create derivative
works from the Provided Data; to display the Provided Data; and to make the
Provided Data available to end users through services or sites."

Anyone running this firmware requests their own free API token from
<https://511.org/open-data/token> and accepts that agreement themselves. **No
token is distributed with this project** — it is entered once through the setup
portal and stored in the device's NVS. There is no token in this repository, in
any commit in its history, or compiled into any binary.

511 data is provided on an **"as is" and "as available"** basis, with no
warranties of any kind. Predictions can be wrong, late, or absent. Do not rely on
this device to catch a train you cannot afford to miss.

- Portal: <https://511.org/open-data/transit>
- Terms: <http://assets.511.org/pdf/nextgen/developers/511_Data_Agreement_Final.pdf>

## 2. Schedule and station data — Caltrain / PCJPB, published by Trillium

**The compiled timetable and station list are Caltrain's data.** They are
generated by `tools/gen_stations.py` and `tools/gen_timetable.py` from the
official Caltrain GTFS static feed:

    https://data.trilliumtransit.com/gtfs/caltrain-ca-us/caltrain-ca-us.zip

published by **[Trillium](https://trilliumtransit.com/)** on Caltrain's behalf.

`src/stations.h` and `src/timetable_data.h` are generated files. They are a
*derivative work* of that feed — a re-encoding of its stops, trips, stop_times
and calendar into a form an ESP32 can query from flash. Their content, including
every station name, stop_id, latitude and published train number, originates with
Caltrain and **not** with this project.

The **Peninsula Corridor Joint Powers Board (PCJPB)** retains ownership:

> "The PCJPB maintains title, ownership, rights and interest in and to Data."

PCJPB grants "non-exclusive, limited and revocable rights to use, reproduce, and
redistribute PCJPB Data," which is what permits those two generated headers to
ship in this repository. The grant is **revocable** and the feed may change or be
withdrawn without notice; the data is provided **"as is" and "as available"**
with no warranties.

- Caltrain developer resources: <https://www.caltrain.com/developer-resources>

## 3. Trademarks — not affiliated with Caltrain or PCJPB

> **This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or approved
> by Caltrain, the Peninsula Corridor Joint Powers Board, the Metropolitan
> Transportation Commission, or 511 SF Bay.**

"Caltrain" is a trademark of the Peninsula Corridor Joint Powers Board. It is
used here **nominatively** — solely to identify the rail service whose departures
this device displays, and the source of the data it displays. No claim is made to
the mark, and no endorsement is implied or should be inferred.

**The Caltrain logo appears nowhere — not in this repository, and not on the
device.** Both agencies restrict the use of their marks and copyrighted materials
in association with their data, and artwork is the clearest case of that.

An earlier version of this firmware could push a bitmap plate onto the boot and
setup screens when a generated header was present. **That code path has been
removed rather than left switched off**, so no build can display a logo: there is
no header to supply, no flag to set, and nothing to accidentally re-enable. Boot
screens are text, and the boot splash carries the disclaimer in §3 above where
every user will see it. CI additionally fails if artwork is ever committed.

Station names such as "San Francisco Caltrain Station Southbound" appear in the
station list and the test fixture because they are the literal stop names in the
GTFS feed and the SIRI response. They are quoted as data, not adopted as branding.
